Gavin

A private personal assistant for one person.

Gavin is a personal assistant bot that its owner runs for themself. It helps them keep track of their week: it reminds them of upcoming plans, sends a short daily brief, and can add or remove calendar events after the owner approves each change.

Gavin is not a public service. It is not offered to, and cannot be used by, anyone other than its owner. It connects to its owner's Google Calendar only, through Google's sign-in, with the owner's permission.
